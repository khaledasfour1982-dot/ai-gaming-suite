Winning the game
You win in chess by checkmating your opponent's king. Checkmate means the king is in check and has no legal move to escape.

Basic movement
Each piece has its own way of moving. Kings move one square in any direction, queens move any number of squares in any direction, rooks move horizontally or vertically, bishops move diagonally, and knights move in an L-shape.

Diagonal moves
Bishops move diagonally any number of squares as long as they are not blocked by other pieces. Pawns capture diagonally one square forward. Queens also move diagonally like bishops.

Check and checkmate
A king is in check if it is attacked by at least one enemy piece. It is checkmate if the king is in check and no legal move removes the threat.

Draws
Games can also end in a draw in several ways, such as stalemate, insufficient material, threefold repetition, or the fifty-move rule.
