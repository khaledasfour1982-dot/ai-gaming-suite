Objective
Two players take turns marking spaces on a 3×3 grid. One player uses X, the other uses O. The goal is to be the first to get three of your marks in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Setup
Start with an empty 3×3 grid. Decide who is X and who is O. X always goes first.

Turns
Players alternate turns. On your turn, place your mark (X or O) in any empty square. You cannot play on an occupied square.

Winning
You win if you have three of your marks in a straight line:
- Any of the three rows
- Any of the three columns
- Either of the two diagonals

Draw
The game is a draw if all nine squares are filled and neither player has three in a row.

Basic strategy tips
- Try to play in the center when possible.
- Block your opponent if they already have two in a row.
- Create “forks” where you threaten two winning lines at once.
